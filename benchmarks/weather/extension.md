# Ten-city reuse check

After measuring the original three cities, we estimated a price crossover at eight cities. To test that projection, we fixed seven further cities before fetching their data or sampling more answers. We will include all ten in the result even if the projected savings fail to materialize.

Use the original three Skeiner and three client-side JavaScript programs unchanged. For each new city run three fresh direct conversations with the original model, tools, prompt and limits. Report accuracy, model spend over all ten cities including the original authorship costs, and the number of upstream reads. The supplementary community SQL study stays at three cities.

All new cities use a 30 °C threshold and ERA5 daily maximum temperatures. To vary the dates as well as the location, use June 1–August 31, 2019 for six northern cities and December 1, 2019–February 29, 2020 for Sydney.
